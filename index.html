What I do

I'm a UI/UX designer who obsesses over how interfaces feel. I design clean systems first, then build prototypes that run. Hover a card to see how I think.

🎨

UI design

Clean layouts, glass and depth, strong hierarchy.

I start with type, spacing and contrast, then add colour and depth only where they guide the eye.
🧭

UX flows

Research, user journeys and wireframes that make sense.

I map the journey first, so every screen has one clear job and one clear next step.
✨

Motion

Micro-interactions that explain, not distract.

Movement should show what changed and where to look next. If it doesn't, it goes.
🧩

Design systems

Tokens, components and consistent patterns.

Reusable pieces keep products consistent and make future changes fast.
⚙️

Build & ship

Python, TypeScript, CI/CD, data and fintech projects.

I take designs into working code, so ideas get tested in real hands, not just in mockups.
