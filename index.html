ECHO

Early Care through Holistic Observation: dementia support and care coordination, built around an explainable support-priority score.

Caregivers log 12 holistic check-in indicators across memory, routine, behaviour, safety and daily living, with consent required before any profile is created. An XGBoost model with SHAP explanations produces a 0–100 ECHO Score, and a human review gate blocks significant interventions until someone accepts the observation. Interventions are tracked as Better, Same or Worse, with a recovery tracker, reminders, a Digital Help Centre and a voice Care Assistant in six languages. The model is trained on synthetic data, and the score is a support indicator, never a diagnosis. Best Hackathon Idea at ARDSICON’26.
